How SurroundLab 5.1 Splits & Processes Channels:

• Multichannel 5.1/6-ch audio: When a native multichannel file is uploaded, SurroundLab bypasses synthesis and feeds discrete channels into the mixer via ChannelSplitterNode(6): CH0: L, CH1: R, CH2: Center, CH3: LFE/Sub, CH4: Rear Left, CH5: Rear Right.

• Stereo / Mono active DSP upmixing: For standard stereo files, high-fidelity matrix decoding is applied: Center (C) extracts in-phase information (L + R) * 0.707 with speech bandpass filtering; Subwoofer (LFE) isolates low-end bass below 120Hz with a Butterworth low-pass filter; Surround Left/Right (SL/SR) computes the stereo out-of-phase difference (L - R) and (R - L) with 18ms Haas psychoacoustic delay and 7kHz atmospheric roll-off.
